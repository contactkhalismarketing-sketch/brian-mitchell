## Connection steps

Open this project’s **Settings → Domains** panel and add `preview.bhavleensingh.com`. The panel will provide the exact DNS record required for this project. Add that record at the DNS provider for `bhavleensingh.com`, wait for verification, and then test the paths listed above.

Do not send the email template until the subdomain is verified. Until then, use the current private project link internally only.
